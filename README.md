📌 Project Overview

This project presents an HR Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee workforce data and identify key factors influencing employee attrition. The dashboard enables HR teams and business leaders to monitor workforce trends, evaluate employee demographics, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze HR data and provide actionable insights that help organizations:
Reduce employee attrition
Improve workforce planning
Identify high-risk departments and job roles
Support strategic HR decision-making

🔍 Key Insights
R&D department records the highest employee attrition.
Employees aged 25–34 years represent the largest workforce segment.
Single employees have the highest attrition compared to married and divorced employees.
Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives experience the highest turnover.
Life Sciences employees contribute the highest attrition among education fields.
Attrition trends vary across age groups and gender, helping identify high-risk employee segments.
